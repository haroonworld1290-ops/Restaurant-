About us

A Taste Worth Coming Back For

Ahmad Food Point & Fish Corner is a local restaurant in Daroghe Wala, Lahore, near Anwar Chanay Wale. We serve fish, BBQ, chicken and parathas, with a menu built around the dishes our neighbourhood loves to eat.

Come for a relaxed meal or take your order with you. We focus on good taste, fresh food and friendly service, and we are open around the clock.
